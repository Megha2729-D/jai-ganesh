BARBEQUE BANQUET

At Jaiganesh Catering Services, we know that every event is special, whether it’s an intimate family gathering or a grand corporate gala. That’s why we’re thrilled to introduce our Barbecue Banquets, a sizzling addition to our catering offerings in Coimbatore. Get ready to indulge in the smoky, savory goodness of a barbecue feast, perfect for events big or small.

Our barbecue menu is a paradise for grill enthusiasts. From tender smoked meats to grilled vegetables, we offer a wide range of barbecue delights that will tantalize your taste buds.Whether it’s a family reunion, a birthday celebration, a corporate team-building event, or a wedding reception, we tailor our barbecue services to match your event’s theme and style.

Elevate the experience with live barbecue stations. Our skilled chefs will grill your selections on the spot, adding an element of excitement and interaction to your event.Quality is non-negotiable for us. We source the finest, locally-sourced ingredients to ensure that every bite is a burst of flavor and freshness.From setup and grilling to serving and cleanup, we handle all aspects of your barbecue banquet. You can relax and enjoy your event while we take care of the rest.

For snacking: chips, salsa, vegetables and dips

On the grill: burgers, hot dogs, chicken, steak, vegetable skewers, corn on the cob and shrimp

On the side: potato salad, pasta salad, french fries, baked potatoes, baked beans and fruit salad.

For dessert: fruit slices, pies , cookies, ice cream sundaes, popsicles and cupcakes.
